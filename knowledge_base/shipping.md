# Shipping

## Orders
Amazon ships all orders. Customers can find delivery estimates and tracking in Amazon Your Orders. We cannot access or change Amazon deliveries.

## Replacement parts
Replacement parts and kits are shipped free from our warehouse. They leave within two business days and usually arrive in three to seven business days. A tracking link is emailed when the parcel ships.

## Countries
Products are sold in the countries where they are listed on Amazon. Replacement parts go to the same address as the original order.
