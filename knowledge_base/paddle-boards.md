# Paddle boards

## Sizes
Choose a board based on the rider's weight, including gear. The 10'6" all-round model suits riders up to 85 kg and beginners on calm water. The 11' all-round model supports up to 110 kg and offers the most stability, including for two light riders. The 12'6" touring model supports up to 130 kg and is faster for longer trips and experienced paddlers. When between sizes, choose the larger board.

## What is in the box
Each board includes an adjustable three-piece paddle, double-action pump with gauge, removable center fin, coiled leash, valve wrench, and carry bag. Replacement part codes: fin PB-FIN, pump PB-PUMP, paddle PB-PADDLE, leash PB-LEASH, wrench PB-WRENCH, and bag PB-BAG.

## Inflation
Inflate the board to 12–15 PSI. Under 12 PSI it may bend in the middle. A pressure drop of 1–2 PSI overnight can happen as the air cools and does not by itself indicate a leak.

## Valve check for slow leaks
Slow leaks are often caused by a loose valve core. Ask the customer to inflate the board to 15 PSI, apply soapy water to the valve, and look for bubbles. If bubbles appear, tighten the core clockwise by a quarter turn with the valve wrench, then test again. Check pressure after 24 hours. If it has dropped by more than 2 PSI, collect the order number, board size, and delivery date.

## Problems and what happens next
Do not promise a shipment; a colleague arranges all shipments. For a missing accessory, the missing part is shipped without requiring a return. For a slow leak, ask the customer to complete the valve check first. If it still leaks within 30 days of delivery, a new board is sent; after 30 days, a patch kit is sent. Escalate split seams to a colleague. To arrange help, collect the order number, board size, and a short description of the issue.
