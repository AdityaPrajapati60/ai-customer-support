# Returns and warranty

## Returns
All purchases are through Amazon, so customers start a return in Amazon Your Orders within 30 days of delivery. Amazon provides the label and manages the refund. We cannot initiate, expedite, or approve returns or refunds. A colleague handles refund questions.

## Warranty
Paddle boards, seams, and valves have a two-year warranty. Paddles, pumps, fins, and leashes have a one-year warranty. Shelving panels and hardware have a two-year warranty. Warranties cover manufacturing faults, not damage from use such as a puncture caused by a rock.

## Other products
For other Harbor & Pine products, returns are handled through Amazon within 30 days of delivery, and manufacturing faults have a two-year warranty.
