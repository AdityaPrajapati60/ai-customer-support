# Shelving

## Models and finishes
All models come in natural oak, white, black, and walnut. HP-S3 is a 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high; HP-S5 is a 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high; HP-C4 is a 2x2 cube unit, 77 cm high; and HP-C9 is a 3x3 cube unit, 112 cm high. The model code appears on the assembly manual and box label.

## Parts lists
Panel stickers show the panel number; identify a panel using the model code and number (for example, HP-S3-04). HP-S3: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 shelf, 5 bottom, 6 back. HP-S5: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4–6 shelves, 7 bottom, 8 back, 9 kick plate. HP-C4: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 center divider, 6 shelf, 7 back. HP-C9: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5–6 dividers, 7–8 shelves, 9 back. Hardware kit HK-S fits S models; HK-C fits C models. Each kit contains cam locks, dowels, screws, and a wall anchor.

## Assembly tips
Lay panels on the box or a rug to prevent scratches. Point each cam lock's arrow toward the cam-bolt hole and turn it clockwise about half a turn until it stops. Install the back panel with its finished side facing inward. Anchor the tall HP-S5 and HP-C9 units to a wall. Assembly typically takes 30–60 minutes for one person; a cordless screwdriver on a low setting is suitable.

## Problems and what happens next
Do not promise a shipment; a colleague arranges all shipments. Missing screws or hardware are handled by sending the model's hardware kit. For one or two damaged panels, the individual panels are sent by part number. For three or more damaged panels or a damaged frame, a full replacement is sent. If the finish is wrong, it is exchanged for the correct one. Collect the order number, model, and damaged panel part numbers; a photo is helpful.
